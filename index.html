How I Work

My Process

01

Discover

Deep dive into the brief — goals, audience, genre, and mood — before writing a single line of code.

02

Design

Wire-frames, sprite sheets, and system diagrams — the entire architecture mapped before build-time.

03

Build

Clean, documented code in the chosen engine. Iterative builds with playable prototypes at every milestone.

04

Polish

Rigorous QA, performance profiling, juice passes, and final fine-tuning until the product feels right.

05

Launch

Deploy, publish, and support. Includes post-launch patches, feedback loops, and optional ongoing updates.
